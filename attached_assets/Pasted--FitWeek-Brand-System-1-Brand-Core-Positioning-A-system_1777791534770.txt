# FitWeek — Brand System

---

## 🧠 1. Brand Core

### 🎯 Positioning
**A system for planning outfits—not a fashion app**

### 🧩 Personality
- Calm, organized, reliable  
- Quietly intelligent (not “AI-heavy”)  
- Minimal and structured  
- Feels like **Notion + Apple + a touch of fashion**

---

## 🎨 2. Color System

### 🌈 Primary Gradient (Signature)
- `#7B61FF → #4DA3FF`

**Usage:**
- Primary buttons (CTAs)
- Highlights
- Active states
- Brand accents

---

### ⚫ Neutral Base

| Token | Color | Usage |
|------|------|------|
| Black | `#0F172A` | Primary text |
| Gray 900 | `#1E293B` | Headers |
| Gray 600 | `#64748B` | Secondary text |
| Gray 300 | `#CBD5E1` | Borders |
| Gray 100 | `#F1F5F9` | Background |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Cards |

**Rule:**  
> 90% neutral, 10% gradient

---

### 🟢 Status Colors

| State | Color | Meaning |
|------|------|--------|
| Clean | `#22C55E` | Available |
| Laundry | `#F97316` | Unavailable |
| Worn | `#64748B` | Recently used |

---

## 🔤 3. Typography

### Primary Font
- **Inter**

### Alternatives
- **Satoshi**
- **General Sans**

---

### Type Scale

| Use | Size | Weight |
|-----|------|-------|
| H1 | 32–40px | Bold |
| H2 | 24–28px | Semi-bold |
| Body | 16px | Regular |
| Caption | 12–14px | Medium |

**Guideline:**  
Keep typography clean and highly readable.

---

## 🧱 4. Spacing System

Use an **8pt grid**

- 8 / 16 / 24 / 32 / 40

### Layout Rules
- Card padding: 16–24px  
- Section spacing: 32–48px  
- Button height: ≥ 44px  

---

## 🔲 5. Component Style

### Cards
- Background: White (`#FFFFFF`)
- Border: `#E2E8F0`
- Radius: 16px
- Shadow: Very subtle

---

### Buttons

#### Primary
- Gradient background
- White text
- Radius: 12–16px

#### Secondary
- Light gray background
- Dark text

---

### Inputs
- Minimal styling
- Subtle blue focus state

---

## 🧩 6. Iconography

### Style
- Outline icons
- Rounded edges
- 2px stroke

### Common Icons
- 👕 Clothing
- 🧺 Laundry
- ☀️🌧️ Weather
- 📅 Calendar

**Rule:**  
Keep icons simple and consistent.

---

## 📱 7. Product Visual Language

### 1. Outfit Composition
- Flat lay layout (top, bottom, shoes)
- Clean spacing
- No clutter

---

### 2. Weekly Grid
- Card per day
- Weather icon at top
- Outfit preview inside

---

### 3. Status Indicators
- Small colored badges/dots
- Never visually dominant

---

## ✨ 8. Motion

### Principles
- Subtle
- Fast
- Functional

### Examples
- Swipe → slight bounce  
- Confirm → soft lock animation  
- Outfit build → fade-in sequence  

---

## 🧭 9. Brand Voice

### Tone
- Simple

- Helpful

---

### Examples

---

### Microcopy

---

## 🧠 10. Brand Philosophy

Most wardrobe apps feel:
- Overdesigned  
- Fashion-heavy  
- Cluttered  

**FitWeek should feel:**
- Clean  
- Structured  
- Calm  
- Predictable  

---

## 🚀 Final Summary

> **FitWeek turns your wardrobe into a system—so you can plan once and wear all week.**
